15.Association, aggregation, and composition?
Answer

Association, aggregation, and composition describe relationships between objects/classes in OOP. They mainly differ in ownership and object lifetime.

A useful way to remember them is:

Association → knows/uses
Aggregation → has, but independently
Composition → has and owns

1. Association

Association is a general relationship where two classes are connected because their objects interact with or use each other.

There is no strong ownership requirement.

Example:

class Teacher:
    def teach(self):
        print("Teaching")


class Student:
    def learn(self):
        print("Learning")


teacher = Teacher()
student = Student()

# Teacher and Student are associated

A teacher interacts with students, but the teacher does not own the student's lifecycle.

Teacher ───── Student
       association

Association can be one-to-one, one-to-many, or many-to-many.

2. Aggregation

Aggregation is a weaker form of "has-a" relationship.

One object contains or groups other objects, but the contained objects can exist independently of the container.

Example:

class Player:
    def __init__(self, name):
        self.name = name


class Team:
    def __init__(self, players):
        self.players = players


p1 = Player("Rahul")
p2 = Player("Aman")

team = Team([p1, p2])

Here:

Team ───── Player
       aggregation

The Team has players, but if the Team object is destroyed, the Player objects can still exist.

So:

Aggregation = has-a, but the parts have independent lifetimes.

3. Composition

Composition is a stronger form of "has-a" relationship.

The containing object owns the contained objects, and their lifecycle is strongly tied to the container.

Example:

class Engine:
    def start(self):
        print("Engine started")


class Car:
    def __init__(self):
        self.engine = Engine()

Here, the Car creates and owns its Engine.

Car ◆──── Engine
      composition

Conceptually:

Composition = has-a + strong ownership.

If the Car is removed, its Engine is considered part of that Car rather than an independently managed object.

Main difference
Feature	Association	Aggregation	Composition
Relationship	General connection	Weak has-a	Strong has-a
Ownership	No required ownership	Weak/shared ownership	Strong ownership
Object lifetime	Independent	Independent	Usually dependent
Dependency	Relatively loose	Loose	Stronger
Example	Teacher ↔ Student	Team → Players	Car → Engine
UML notation	Simple line	Hollow diamond ◇	Filled diamond ◆
Easy analogy

Think about a company:

Company ───── Employee

Association:
The company and employee are related.

Aggregation:

Department ◇──── Employee

A department has employees, but an employee can exist even if that department is removed.

Composition:

House ◆──── Room

A room is considered a constituent part of a particular house and its lifecycle is strongly tied to that house in the model.

Important distinction

The most important interview point is object lifetime and ownership:

Association
    ↓
Objects are related

Aggregation
    ↓
Container has objects
but objects can exist independently

Composition
    ↓
Container owns objects
and their lifetimes are strongly connected
Interview takeaway

Association means objects are related, aggregation means one object has other objects but they can exist independently, and composition means one object strongly owns its constituent objects.
