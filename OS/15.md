15.How do you calculate waiting time and turnaround time?
These are two important metrics used to evaluate CPU scheduling algorithms.

1. Turnaround Time

Turnaround time is the total time taken by a process from its arrival/submission until its completion.

Formula
$$ \boxed{\text{Turnaround Time} = \text{Completion Time} - \text{Arrival Time}} $$

Where:

Completion Time (CT) = time when the process finishes
Arrival Time (AT) = time when the process enters the ready queue
2. Waiting Time

Waiting time is the total time a process spends waiting in the ready queue for the CPU.

Formula
$$ \boxed{\text{Waiting Time} = \text{Turnaround Time} - \text{Burst Time}} $$

Where:

Burst Time (BT) = total CPU time required by the process

Therefore:

$$ \boxed{WT = CT - AT - BT} $$
Example

Suppose we have:

Process	Arrival Time	Burst Time	Completion Time
P1	0	5	5
P2	1	3	8
P1

Turnaround time:

$$ TAT = CT - AT $$ $$ = 5 - 0 = 5 $$

Waiting time:

$$ WT = TAT - BT $$ $$ = 5 - 5 = 0 $$

So:

P1:
Turnaround Time = 5
Waiting Time    = 0
P2

Turnaround time:

$$ TAT = 8 - 1 = 7 $$

Waiting time:

$$ WT = 7 - 3 = 4 $$

So:

P2:
Turnaround Time = 7
Waiting Time    = 4
Visualizing it

Assume FCFS:

Time →   0    1         5        8
         |----|---------|--------|
              P1        P2

P2 arrives at time 1, but P1 is using the CPU until time 5.

Therefore P2 waits:

$$ 5 - 1 = 4 $$

Then P2 executes for 3 units and completes at 8.

So:

$$ TAT_{P2} = 8 - 1 = 7 $$

and:

$$ WT_{P2} = 7 - 3 = 4 $$
Summary
Metric	Formula	Meaning
Turnaround Time	CT − AT	Total time from arrival to completion
Waiting Time	TAT − BT	Time spent waiting for CPU
Waiting Time	CT − AT − BT	Equivalent formula

For multiple processes, we can also calculate average waiting time and average turnaround time:

$$ \text{Average WT} = \frac{\sum WT}{n} $$ $$ \text{Average TAT} = \frac{\sum TAT}{n} $$
Interview takeaway

Turnaround time measures the total time from arrival to completion, while waiting time measures how long a process waits for CPU time. The key formulas are TAT = CT − AT and WT = TAT − BT.
