23.What are the four necessary conditions for deadlock?
A deadlock can occur only when all four of the following conditions exist simultaneously. These are called the Coffman conditions.

1. Mutual Exclusion

At least one resource must be non-shareable, meaning only one process/thread can use it at a time.

Example:
Only one thread can hold a particular mutex.

2. Hold and Wait

A process/thread is holding at least one resource while waiting to acquire another resource.

Thread 1:
    holds Lock A
    waits for Lock B
3. No Preemption

A resource cannot be forcibly taken away from the process holding it. It must be released voluntarily.

Thread 1 holds Lock A
→ another thread cannot simply take Lock A away
4. Circular Wait

A circular chain of processes/threads exists where each one is waiting for a resource held by the next.

Thread 1 → waits for Lock B → held by Thread 2
Thread 2 → waits for Lock A → held by Thread 1

This creates:

T1 → T2 → T1
Easy way to remember

M-H-N-C

Mutual exclusion
Hold and wait
No preemption
Circular wait

Interview takeaway

All four conditions must hold simultaneously for a deadlock to occur. Breaking even one of them can prevent deadlock.
